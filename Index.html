Our History

Runaba Secondary School was established in 2005 to bring quality education to the heart of Burera District. Starting with only 89 students and 5 teachers,on this time boys were 86 and 3 girls.our school was started with ordinary level only the school has since grown into a fully equipped learning institution with hundreds of students and a dedicated staff.
